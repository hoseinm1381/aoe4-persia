# Persian Market — unbuilt source draft

Recipe/README only: no Blender execution, saved master, textures, source states/LODs, validation, cook or integration is claimed.

The ordinary Market is a short publicly permeable commercial lane, inward-facing merchant bays and modest goods stores. A2 has three asymmetric open timber/brick stalls and a partial lane shade. A3 has four vaulted shops and a broader divided goods store. A4 has six regular shop bays, a higher trading spine and two separate small stores. This is a linear local market, without the Samarkand Bazaar landmark's central dome, four vaulted arms or sixteen-room plan. It remains plainer and lower; actual comparative visual hierarchy must still be inspected by the parent.
