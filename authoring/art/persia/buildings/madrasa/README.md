# Persian Madrasa — unbuilt source draft

Recipe/README only. No Blender execution, master, texture generation, source validation, state/LOD production, cook or native acceptance is claimed.

The original plan is an asymmetric inward teaching court with seven small study cells, an offset shallow teaching loggia, a west library, east writing store and a modest entrance. Low desks, writing boards, mats and compact book bundles identify scholarly use. There is no rear reading dome, deep elliptical central iwan, medical/herb programme, grand mosque sanctuary, minaret or royal four-iwan compound. These choices distinguish it from Gundeshapur, the ordinary Mosque and Shah Masjid Wonder; actual same-camera visual comparison remains parent work.

Historical grounding follows the ordinary-building guide: [Archnet, Ghiyathiyya at Khargird](https://www.archnet.org/sites/4726?media_content_id=41092), [Getty's Timurid madrasa record](https://www.getty.edu/cona/CONAFullSubject.aspx?subid=700048641) and [Iranica, Isfahan madrasas](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/isfahan-x4-madrasas/). The Khargird reference belongs to 1438–44/1444; the later Chaharbagh Madrasa is 1704–10 and is not presented as in-period evidence. The recipe is an original compact game adaptation, not a measured Khargird reconstruction or a claim that this arrangement existed in 934. No fabricated Persian calligraphy is authored.
