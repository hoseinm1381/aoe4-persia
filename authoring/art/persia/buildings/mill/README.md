## Editing and reconstruction

Use the configured Python and E:/Blender5.2/blender.exe from the task environment. From the temporary workspace, source generation is:

    blender.exe --background --factory-startup --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python art/persia/buildings/_shared/build_family.py -- mill

Run verify_family.py in a fresh --background --factory-startup process for saved readback, and add --rebuild for cold reconstruction. Rendering opens the saved master with render_family.py -- mill healthy, coverage or closeups. review_sheets.py mill MODE assembles existing pixels and verifies coverage/closeup hashes.

Generation refuses to overwrite a master whose SHA differs from its owned receipt. Direct manual Blender edits therefore remain protected. Preserve direct edits and reconcile them into recipe.py/lods.py before requesting a scripted rebuild; do not bypass the SHA guard.
